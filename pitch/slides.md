# Opener — three slides, 30 seconds at most

Then go straight to the live app. Blanks (`__`) are filled at 3:15 or the line is cut.

---

## 1 · Why

**About half of applicants fall out.**
About half of those leave the hospital early.
*— IHH's representative, today. Their estimate, not an audit.*

A hospital bed isn't housing.

> Say (10 s): "IHH's representative estimated this morning that about half of applicants fall
> out, and about half of those leave the hospital early."

---

## 2 · What

**ICA, Referral Readiness: for IHH's referral coordinator.**

**Prepare** the referral · **Resolve** the fax · **Coordinate** every open issue

The docket, then one screen with four boxes:
**What's missing** · **Needs LVN review** · **What we know (with pages)** · **Next action**

Every finding: its quote, its page, and a person's Checked / Wrong. **The coordinator decides.**

ICA drafts. It never sends and never decides.

> Say (10 s): "ICA turns a fax into what's missing and what needs the LVN, with pages, and turns
> each open issue into one item with an owner. The coordinator decides."

---

## 3 · Proof

- On IHH's 11 real referrals, ICA's lean matched IHH's decision on **__ of 11**. *A lean, not a
  decision.*
- Every quote is checked against the page it cites. **__** didn't match and were set aside.
- Not in the packet means **unknown**. Dates blacked out means **can't tell**.

> Say (10 s): "Here's how often it agreed with IHH, and here's where it didn't. Let me show you."
